## About the Project

**Salle d'Or: The Vigil** is a site-specific mixed reality installation exploring climate disaster, displacement, and dissociation. Audiences are cast as FEMA Assessors surveying a home destroyed by Hurricane Helene — and through Quest 3 "Spectra-3 Digital Binoculars," they uncover the memories of two children who sheltered inside.

*"Survey the memories of a life left behind."*

## The Deck

Covers:
- The problem with VR + traditional theater
- The Spectra-3 handheld AR solution
- The physical set: Appalachian Clay
- Narrative architecture: 3 Loops of Truth (Vivid Wish / Golden Lie / Derelict Truth)
- The Tactile Triple Read — one object, three realities
- Grounded in Hurricane Helene (2024)
- The Vigil ending
- Why now / call to action
